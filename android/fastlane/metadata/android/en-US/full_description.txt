Gym Tracker is a simple workout log for the gym. Log sets, reps and weight in a few taps and keep an honest history of every session.
It works fully offline, with no account needed. Sign in only if you want a backup and sync across your devices.

LOG FAST
• Start from a template and your sets are already laid out.
• Rest timer between sets, with an optional buzz when rest is over.
• Set a default rest time for each exercise.
• Control your workout from the lock screen notification.
• Left mid-workout? Resume it, or finish at your last set.

PLANS AND TEMPLATES
• Starter plans you rotate through, day by day, plus ready-made workouts.
• Not sure where to start? Answer three questions and get a suggested plan.
• Build your own templates for any split: full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs.
• Start an empty workout and add exercises as you go.
• Save a finished workout as a new template.

EXERCISE LIBRARY
• A growing library of exercises with main muscles, equipment and how-to steps.
• Add your own exercises and keep a note on each one (seat height, grip, cues).
• Hide the ones you never do.

MORE THAN WEIGHT AND REPS
Each exercise is logged as what it is:
• Weight and reps for barbell, dumbbell and machine lifts
• Reps only for pull-ups, push-ups and dips
• Time for planks and holds, with optional added weight
• Distance and time for runs and rows
• Distance with weight for carries like the farmer's walk

PROGRESS AND HISTORY
• Weekly totals at a glance.
• A chart of your heaviest set each session, for any lift you pick.
• Every past workout in one list. Search it or filter by exercise.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• Your workouts are saved on your phone, so this workout log works offline, even at a gym with no signal.
• Make an account (Google or email) only if you want a backup and sync across devices.
• Export a backup file whenever you like.
• Delete your account and data from inside the app.
• Crash reports help fix bugs.

A TOOL, NOT A NETWORK
No social feed, no followers, nothing to share. Just your training.

FOR BEGINNERS AND EXPERIENCED LIFTERS
A workout tracker for both. New to strength training? Follow a starter plan and the how-to for each exercise. Lifting for years? Build your own templates and the app stays out of your way.
