# Delete your {app} account

{app} is made by Rukavina (Ivan Karlo Rukavina, Croatia). This page
explains how to delete your {app} account and the data stored with it.

## In the app

1. Open the **You** tab.
2. Scroll down to **Account** and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

Your account and all synced data are deleted right away.

## Without the app

Email [privacy@rukavina.app](mailto:privacy@rukavina.app?subject=Delete%20my%20Gym%20Tracker%20account)
**from the email address you use for your account**, with the subject
"Delete my {app} account". We only act on requests from that address,
so nobody else can delete your account.

We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply
when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your account and sign-in (email address, Google sign-in link)
- Your profile, workouts, plans, templates, custom exercises and notes
- Your body weight entries
- The record of your health-data consent

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Database backups** still hold the data until they age out, at
  most 30 days after deletion.
- **Crash reports** may contain your user id, never workout or body
  data. Firebase deletes them after 90 days.
- **Server logs** (IP addresses, and the user id of the deletion
  itself) are overwritten as new ones come in, usually within a few
  weeks.

## Using {app} without an account

Without an account nothing is stored on our servers. Your data is
only on your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android
settings, to delete it.

More in the [privacy policy]({base}/privacy).
