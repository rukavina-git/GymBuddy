# {app} privacy policy

{app} is a workout log for Android. This policy says what data the
app and its servers handle, why, and what you can do about it.

## Who is responsible

Ivan Karlo Rukavina, Croatia, is the controller of your data.
Privacy questions and requests: [privacy@rukavina.app](mailto:privacy@rukavina.app).

## Without an account

You can use {app} without an account. Your workouts, plans, body
weight and profile then stay on your phone. Nothing of it is sent to
us. Crash reports are still sent (see below).

## With an account

When you create an account we store:

- **Account:** your email address and a user id from Firebase
  Authentication. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your
  email address, name and a link to your Google profile picture.
- **Profile:** the name, avatar, experience and any optional details
  you enter (bio, height, birth date, gender, activity level, goal,
  target weight, training days).
- **Training data:** your workouts, sets, plans and which one is
  active, custom exercises, your notes and favourites on exercises,
  and body weight entries.
- **Consent record:** when you agreed to us storing health data, and
  which version of the wording you saw.

Workouts and body data count as health data under the GDPR. We store
them only with your explicit consent, given on the "Keep a copy on our
servers?" screen when you create an account (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). The
rest we need to run your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

An account is optional: the app works fully without one. To create
one you need an email address (or a Google account); everything else
is up to you.

We use this data only to back up and sync your account across your
devices. We don't profile you or make automated decisions about you. We don't sell it, show ads based on it, or share it with
anyone except the providers below.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report through
Firebase Crashlytics: the error, the app version, the phone model and
Android version, and, if you are signed in, your user id. Crash
reports contain no workout or body data. We use them to fix bugs, which
is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Crash reports are part of how the app works and can't be turned off:
without them we can't find and fix bugs. If you don't want them sent,
please don't use the app. Questions or objections:
[privacy@rukavina.app](mailto:privacy@rukavina.app).

## Server logs

Our servers log requests, including IP addresses, to keep the service
running and secure (legitimate interest). The logs are limited in size
and are overwritten as new ones come in, usually within a few weeks.

## Providers

These companies process data for us. netcup and Google do so under a
data processing agreement:

| Provider | What for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| netcup GmbH | Server and database | Germany |
| Google (Firebase Authentication, Crashlytics) | Sign-in, crash reports | EU and USA |
| GitHub (Microsoft) | Database backups, encrypted before upload so GitHub can't read them | USA |

Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the
EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete the account. Backups of the
database are kept for 30 days, so deleted data is gone from them after
30 days at most. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.

## Your rights

You can:

- **See and take your data:** export it in the app (You → Export data), or
  ask us by email.
- **Correct it:** edit it in the app.
- **Delete it:** delete your account in the app (You → Delete
  account). This removes your account and all synced data at once.
  Without the app, follow [these steps]({base}/delete-account).
- **Withdraw consent:** deleting the account withdraws it, in the app
  or by email from your account's address. Data on your phone stays
  there until you erase it or uninstall the app.
- **Object, restrict, or ask about anything else:** email us.

You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority,
AZOP ([azop.hr](https://azop.hr)), or the authority where you live.

## Age

{app} is for people aged 16 and over.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change
affects what we do with health data, the app asks for your consent
again.
