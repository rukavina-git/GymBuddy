the loop. There is no written banned list. Without them, rounds drift to generic AI UI. PRODUCT.md
still lists the old palette (midnight #030407, cobalt #1558C9).

   Claude's and impeccable's ideas, refined step by step. Lives in design-brief.md under

   write`, kept under `.impeccable/` (not committed). A short
   summary goes into design-brief.md.
4. **PRODUCT.md.** Brand Commitments updated to the locked palette
   and type. Nothing else changes.

## Tests
None (no code). impeccable `critique` + native `audit` on the option

style). Claude checks nothing overlaps or floats outside a section.
