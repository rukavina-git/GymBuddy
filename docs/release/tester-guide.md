# Gym Tracker: tester guide

Thanks for testing! It takes 2 minutes to join.

## Join
1. Open this link on your Android phone, signed in with the Google
   account you gave me:
   https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.rukavina.gymbuddy
2. Tap **Become a tester**.
3. Install Gym Tracker from Google Play:
   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rukavina.gymbuddy
   If Play shows an error, wait a few minutes and try again. Right
   after joining, it takes Google a moment to let you in.

## Stay in for 14 days
Google only counts you if you stay opted in for 14 days in a row.
Please don't leave the test or uninstall the app before then. Leaving
and joining again restarts the count.

## What to try
- Create an account (or continue as a guest).
- Log a workout: pick exercises, add sets, finish it.
- Look at your history and progress.
- Open the app a few times over the 14 days, even for a quick look.

## Feedback
In the app: **You → About → Send feedback**. It opens an email to
the developer. Anything helps: bugs, confusing screens, ideas.
Screenshots are welcome.

## Privacy
Privacy policy: https://gymtracker.rukavina.app/privacy
You can delete your account in the app (You → Delete account) at any time.
